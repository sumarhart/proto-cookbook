In these notebooks we used ERA5 data from Earth Data Hub to look at monthly precipitation anomalies over a given area. The first example we went over (notebook1) looked at the monthly anomalies at Moundville, Alabama where we used a barchart to compare the positive (wetter) vs negative (dryer) anomalies from 1979-2025. In the second notebook (notebook2), we took a closer look at the same data and created a map of the entire state of Alabama for a specific month and year. April 1979 was chosen because it was the period of a historical flood for Tuscaloosa and Moundville, AL. Because the map showed increased precipitaion anomalies in that region compared to the rest of the state, it shows that ERA5 is able to capture this type of event. This could be further examined on other historical events and potentially compared to paleoclimate records to have a better understanding of extreme flood frequency.
